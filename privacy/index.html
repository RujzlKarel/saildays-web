Saildays Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

In short: the developer of Saildays does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party crash reporting. Your trips stay on your phone.

Who we are

Saildays is an Android app published by Nice One s.r.o. (company ID 14033453), Brno, Czech Republic, which is the controller for the purposes of this policy. Contact: privacy@saildays.app.

Location

Saildays uses your device location (permission "While using the app") only while you record a trip: from the moment you start recording until you end the trip. While recording, a notification is always visible. The location is used to draw your track and to suggest log entries (course change, anchoring, departure). It is processed on your phone and stored in the app database on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Saildays does not request background location access.

Notifications

Notifications show that recording is running and ask you to confirm suggested log entries. They are created on your phone.

Backup to your Google account

If Android backup is turned on on your phone, Android may back up the Saildays trip database to your own Google account, so your trips can be restored on a new phone. Your trips may also be transferred to a new phone directly during setup (Android device transfer). The backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we have no access to it. Backups of this app are not restricted to devices with a screen lock, so on a device without a screen lock the backup may not be end-to-end encrypted. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

Export

Only when you tap Export, Saildays creates a ZIP file with your trip (a GPX track and a JSON log). It goes only where you choose: an app you share it with (for example Google Drive or e-mail) or a folder on your phone. From then on, the privacy policy of the app or service you chose applies.

Advertising, analytics, crash statistics

Saildays contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting code. Google Play may collect anonymous crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) according to your device settings; that is done by Google, not by us.

Deleting your data

All data is on your phone. Uninstalling Saildays or clearing its storage deletes it. Backups in your Google account can be managed in your Google account settings.

Children

Saildays is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
